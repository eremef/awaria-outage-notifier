### WiK Opole: observed data and mapping

The category-11 WordPress REST request returned HTTP 200 with `X-WP-Total: 5` and `X-WP-TotalPages: 1`. Each post exposes `id`, `date`, `date_gmt`, `link`, `title.rendered`, `content.rendered`, and `categories`; the full notice is in paragraph HTML. The general RSS feed exists but mixes categories, so the category-filtered REST response is the simpler source. The five posts observed span 22 June–19 October 2026: three water-cut notices and two roadworks notices. Category membership alone is insufficient to classify an outage. No retention policy was found; honor the pagination headers and bound fetched pages, but do not infer a fixed retention period from this sample.

Representative official notices: [Krótka/Wiejska](https://wikopole.com.pl/planowana-przerwa-w-dostawie-wody-3/) (`id=7377`, 20–23 October, 08:00–13:00); [Głogowska/Zielonogórska/Kaszubska](https://wikopole.com.pl/planowana-przerwa-w-dostawie-wody-2/) (`id=7375`, 22 October, 08:00–14:00); and [Czarnowąska/Przyjazna](https://wikopole.com.pl/planowana-przerwa-w-dostawie-wody/) (`id=7373`, 21 October, 09:00–13:00). Some streets have house-number lists or ranges (`Wiejska 145`, `Głogowska 1–19/14–22`, `Zielonogórska 1–1E/2–6C`, `Kaszubska 9/10/12`, `Czarnowąska 6–8`). The time separator can be a dot (`08.00`); dates use day-month-year Polish notation. Text may also contain water-quality boilerplate, which must remain in the whole message without being mistaken for another outage.

Map `id` plus any proven occurrence to a stable alert identity, `link` to source evidence if the current `UnifiedAlert` contract gains a link, `content.rendered` to the complete readable message, and explicit notice dates/times to `startDate` and `endDate` as local `Europe/Warsaw` wall time. `date`/`date_gmt` are publication metadata only. Extract locality and street groups separately from the prose; do not match a street under the wrong locality. Planned/failure classification comes from outage wording, and roadwork-only posts are skipped. The 20–23 October notice says "w dniach ... od godz. 08.00 do godz. 13.00" but never explicitly says "each day"; do not silently represent it as either daily windows or a continuous four-day outage. Keep the exact schedule text, and settle the interval mapping before implementation.

### ECO SA: observed data and mapping

The page is server-rendered and advertises the RSS feed above (an Atom variant also exists). RSS returned HTTP 200 and `application/rss+xml`, with full HTML notice text in each `<description>`. The current feed/list contains one item, [Krapkowice (20.10.2026)](https://www.ecosa.pl/strefa-klienta/przerwy-w-dostawie-ciepla/krapkowice.html). Its `<link>` and `<guid>` identify the article. It says a main-network failure stopped heat on **20 October 2026 at 10:00** for all buildings on system heat in Krapkowice; restoration is **expected 21 October**, without an hour. The item `<pubDate>` is **27 August 2022**, clearly unsuitable as the outage start or freshness signal. `?start=1` produced an empty feed and `?limit=50` still one item. The observed feed is a current-notice surface; archive depth and retention are unconfirmed.

Other official article examples show formats to support: [Kęty](https://www.ecosa.pl/strefa-klienta/przerwy-w-dostawie-ciepla/kety.html), a planned 17–21 August 2026 citywide interruption; [Lubliniec](https://www.ecosa.pl/strefa-klienta/przerwy-w-dostawie-ciepla/lubliniec.html), planned 6 August 08:00–18:00 and citywide; [Malbork](https://www.ecosa.pl/strefa-klienta/przerwy-w-dostawie-ciepla/malbork.html), two date intervals with multiple street/building bullets. Convert description HTML to text while preserving paragraph, line-break, and list boundaries. Use the title/body for city, planned/failure status, affected streets/buildings, cause, and explicit schedule; keep the whole body for both local and other alerts. Treat citywide notices as matching that city without inventing streets or house numbers. A date-only *expected* restoration must not be shown as an exact restoration hour. Choose a bounded expiry rule for such notices during implementation and retain the source's uncertainty in the message.

## Proposed repository changes

### Rust and matching

- Add `src-tauri/src/wik_opole.rs` and `src-tauri/src/eco_sa.rs`, each implementing `AlertProvider`, using Awaria's existing HTTP clients, `serde`/`quick-xml`/`scraper` and date/address helpers as applicable. Follow recent water providers such as `pwik_olsztyn.rs` and heat providers such as `energa_cieplo_kaliskie.rs`; introduce no shared abstraction unless both sources reveal a real common need.
- In `src-tauri/src/api_logic.rs`, add both `AlertSource` variants and their Serde names, display names, water/heat notification category, `Display` mapping, and service-area predicates. Resolve WiK locality aliases against TERYT and Opolskie (city Opole has SIMC `965016`), without expanding water eligibility to wastewater-only communes. For ECO, the four verified notice cities are Krapkowice, Kęty, Lubliniec, and Malbork; this is not a complete heat-service roster. Avoid both an Opole-only predicate and a province-wide local match. A coarse fetch gate may be broader than exact notice matching if needed to avoid missing a new ECO city, while notice-locality matching stays exact.
- In `src-tauri/src/lib.rs`, declare/register the providers and use the existing alert filtering path. Parse notice-locality plus affected street groups before setting `address_index`/`is_local`; citywide ECO notices may match an active address in that city. Preserve the full message. Keep nonlocal `location` to city/locality only. Put all house-number enforcement behind `Settings.filter_by_house_no` in `apply_alert_filtering`; when enabled, use the street-associated numbers and existing matching helpers, including ranges and suffixes. Do not enforce numbers unconditionally in the provider.
- Inspect stable hash/deduplication and stale-alert filtering for repeat windows, updated notice bodies, RSS items with old `pubDate`, date-only expected restoration, and notices that remain on the site after expiry. Do not use publication time as an outage time.

### Frontend, daemon, Android, and docs

- `public/script.js`: add the two sources in water/heat groups, alphabetically by ID within each group; add only needed region predicates and align them with Rust's service-area rules. Reuse the current alert-card routing and category styling. `public/i18n.js`: add Polish and English names/short names. `public/awaria-card.js`: add source metadata only if its current lookup needs explicit entries; no provider-specific color.
- `src-tauri/src/bin/awaria_daemon.rs`: include each variant in the correct utility mapping; provider registration should make both available to the daemon. `awaria_outage_monitor/config.yaml` and the add-on `README.md`/`README.pl.md`: allow and document both IDs.
- The current product has single-source and aggregate widgets, so add `WikOpoleWidgetProvider.kt` and `EcoSaWidgetProvider.kt` following current `BaseWidgetProvider` patterns, with `@xml/widget_single_info`. Update `AndroidManifest.xml`, `BaseWidgetProvider.kt` source names/types/layout selection, `WidgetConfigActivity.kt`, `WidgetUpdateWorker.kt`, and heat/water sets in `AllWidgetProvider.kt` and `TriWidgetProvider.kt`. Add Polish/English provider and widget labels in `values/strings.xml` and `values-en/strings.xml`. Keep widget display restricted to `is_local == Some(true)`.
- Update provider lists and coverage descriptions in root `README.md` and `README.pl.md`, the add-on docs, and any other affected Markdown specs found during implementation. This checkout currently has no root `docs/` directory.

The checkout already contains staged, unrelated PWiK Olsztyn changes. Preserve them and integrate against their current interfaces without staging, committing, or pushing this plan.

## Review points

1. Confirm that a single `eco_sa` source should represent the ECO group bulletin, including articles about subsidiaries such as ECO Malbork. The evidence points to a group-wide bulletin, but only Krapkowice, Kęty, Lubliniec, and Malbork have been verified from notices; a complete heat-only city roster is unknown.
2. Confirm how Awaria should display and expire an ECO notice whose restoration is only an estimated date. The proposed behavior preserves the date and uncertainty in the message and avoids inventing an exact hour.
3. Decide how to represent WiK's ambiguous "20–23 October, 08:00–13:00" schedule. The text does not specify "daily"; neither daily alerts nor a continuous interval should be assumed without corroboration.

**Review gate:** Approved by the user. Current implementation choices: `eco_sa` fetches the shared bulletin for all configured locations and matches each alert by the notice's city, because a complete heat-service roster is unavailable; WiK's ambiguous multi-day notice exposes date-only bounds and retains its exact hours in the full message; ECO date-only expected restoration stays date-only in display and uses the stated day's end only for expiry.
